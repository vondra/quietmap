# Australia

[Oceania](/about/oceania)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road keeps a fixed estimate per road class and lane.
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 0.8 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 1.6 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; lorries and buses at most 100 km/h; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 100 outside (traffic drives 84 on a two-way one).
- **Railways:** Timetables on 9,502 of 42,151 km of railway; the lines without one a fixed estimate per kind of line.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 49 heat pumps and 59 air conditioners; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
