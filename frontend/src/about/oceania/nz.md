# New Zealand

[Oceania](/about/oceania)

- **Roads:** Counted by NZTA's and Auckland Transport's counts of 2024 on 27 % of the main roads by length and 1,730 km of smaller roads. Of the main roads, 52 % follow what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 3.4 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median); electric cars 2.3 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; lorries and buses at most 90 km/h; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 100 outside (traffic drives 84 on a two-way one).
- **Railways:** No timetables: a fixed estimate per kind of line.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 49 heat pumps and 59 air conditioners (the region's median); Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
