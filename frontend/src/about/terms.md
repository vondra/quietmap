The map, its tiles and its API are provided as a **service**, free to use and embed with
attribution.

- **You may** link to quietmap.org and embed the map or screenshots in your articles, apps
  and projects, with a visible credit "**quietmap.org**" linking back here, plus
  "© OpenStreetMap contributors" and the base map's credit from the map's corner.
- **You may not** bulk-download tiles, scrape or mirror the dataset, or republish a copy of
  the map as your own service. Raw model data is not distributed.
- **Commercial or high-volume use:** [talk to us first](mailto:info@quietmap.org) — we're
  friendly. Some of our sources are licensed for non-commercial use only, so we need to
  look at each case.

Noise values are model estimates, not measurements; the [validation](/about/validation) page
shows how far to trust them. **No warranty.** quietmap.org is offered as-is, for
information and orientation — please don't rely on it alone for legal, health, safety or
property decisions.

<summary>Privacy</summary>

quietmap.org uses **no trackers and no analytics scripts**, and sets one cookie only when you sign in, to keep you signed in. That is why there is no consent banner to click away.

Like almost every website, our server keeps a standard technical **access log** — IP
address, browser, and requested URLs, including the map coordinates you click — for
security and operations (legitimate interest, GDPR art. 6(1)(f)). Logs rotate automatically
and are kept for at most **30 days**. From them we derive anonymous, aggregated statistics
(visitor countries, browser types, popular areas of the map) — never profiles of
individual visitors, and nothing from the logs is shared with third parties.

The base map loads from CARTO, with OpenTopoMap's terrain or Esri's satellite images when you
pick them; each sees your IP address and the area you view. If you turn on places to stay, our
server asks Stay22 for rooms in that area for your dates and number of guests, without your IP
address; the places' photos load from other sites, and an offer's link is an affiliate link.

Your precise location is used only if you tap the locate button and grant your browser's
permission prompt. While you are signed out, the last five places you opened and the names you give
places stay in your browser.

**Signing in** is optional: it keeps your named places on every device you sign in on, and your last
five places as the device you used last saved them. Our server then keeps your e-mail address, those
names and those places, until you delete the account at the map's top right, which deletes them at
once; our two backup copies, on our own disks, drop them at their next nightly update (GDPR art.
6(1)(b), the service you asked for). The places your browser kept before you signed in stay in
it, and are added to the account only if you say so.
The sign-in link is mailed by [Resend](https://resend.com), a mail service in the United States, which keeps the mail and
its delivery log for 30 days. An address that asked for a link is kept for an hour, to limit how
many links it gets, and is never used for anything else.

**Recordings and photos** you make at a place while signed in are kept on our server with your account,
and only you can open them, until you delete them or the account, which deletes them at once, and
from the backup copies at their next nightly update. A
recording keeps everything it heard, voices nearby too, with the browser, screen size, microphone name
and sound processing the browser reports, to compute its levels later. A photo is kept without its
camera metadata, the position among it: your phone redraws it, and our server removes any left.

Questions: [info@quietmap.org](mailto:info@quietmap.org).
