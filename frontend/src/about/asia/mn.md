## Roads

No open traffic counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world estimate per lane; smaller roads carry the trips their buildings make, else class defaults ([world defaults](/about/methodology)).

Local streets: traffic is derived from the buildings served; motorcycles 2%, from 0.4 × the two-wheeler share of registered vehicles (WHO 2023 country profile, 2021 fleet).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, the medium and heavy vehicles that counted roads of the same class carry; motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 60 km/h in towns, 80 outside (traffic drives 68 on a two-way one).

## Railways

Ulaanbaatar Railway publishes no timetable feed. The Trans-Mongolian line uses the [world railway defaults](/about/methodology).

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, no wind farms.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Other industrial areas: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 146 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Mosques call five times a day.
