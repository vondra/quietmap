# Mongolia

[Asia](/about/asia)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road keeps a fixed estimate per road class and lane.
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); without a speed sign a primary road 60 km/h in towns, 80 outside (traffic drives 68 on a two-way one); the day, evening and night shares as measured in Japan.
- **Railways:** No timetables: a fixed estimate per kind of line.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 146 air conditioners (the region's median); mosques call five times a day.
