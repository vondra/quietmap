# Israel

[Asia](/about/asia)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road keeps a fixed estimate per road class and lane.
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 0.7 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 4.2 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 80 outside (traffic drives 68 on a two-way one), a motorway 110.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 857 of 1,655 km of railway; the lines without one a fixed estimate per kind of line.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 16 air conditioners (a world value); mosques call five times a day.
