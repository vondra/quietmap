[Asia](/about/asia)

- **Roads:** Counted by MLIT's road traffic census of 2021 on 50 % of the main roads by length. Of the main roads, 50 % keep a fixed estimate per road class and lane.
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 2 % of a town street's traffic (a local estimate); electric cars 0.5 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; lorries and buses at most 90 km/h; without a speed sign a primary road 40 km/h in towns, 50 outside, a motorway 100; the day, evening and night shares measured here (the road census, 2021).
- **Railways:** No timetables: a fixed estimate per kind of line.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 237 air conditioners; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
