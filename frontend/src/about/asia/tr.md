# Turkey

[Asia](/about/asia)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road keeps a fixed estimate per road class and lane.
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 2.8 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 1.3 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (traffic drives 76 on a two-way one), a motorway 120.
- **Railways:** No timetable: the lines carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024).
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 16 air conditioners (a world value); five calls a day and Friday's sala.
