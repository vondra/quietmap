## Roads

Kazakhstan publishes no open traffic counts. Traffic is assigned by road class and city; the values are below the world default.

| Road class | Open country | 14 regional cities (×1.4) | Almaty and Astana (×2.0) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Motorway | 20,000 | 28,000 | 40,000 |
| Trunk | 8,000 | 11,200 | 16,000 |
| Primary | 4,000 | 5,600 | 8,000 |
| Secondary | 2,000 | 2,800 | 4,000 |
| Tertiary | 800 | 1,120 | 1,600 |
| Residential | 350 | 490 | 700 |

Each city is a box around its centre, not its administrative boundary.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, the medium and heavy vehicles that counted roads of the same class carry; motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 60 km/h in towns, 100 outside, a motorway 140.

## Railways

No open timetable. Train counts are assigned per corridor:

| Track | Passenger | Freight |
|---|---:|---:|
| Within 20 km of the Pavlodar to Ekibastuz to Karaganda coal line | 2 | 30 |
| Within 18 km of the Almaty to Astana line | 8 | 20 |
| Other main lines | 2 | 10 |
| Branches | 1 | 4 |
| Industrial sidings | 0 | 6 |

Tram and light-rail track gets 80 trains per day in Almaty, 50 in Astana and 60 elsewhere. Surface metro sections are included; see the [railway method](/about/methodology).

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, wind farms excluded.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Oil fields and metal smelters not in those lists: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (a world value). Mosques call five times a day.
