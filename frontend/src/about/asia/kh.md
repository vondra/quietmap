## Roads

No open traffic counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world estimate per lane; smaller roads carry the trips their buildings make, else class defaults ([world defaults](/about/methodology)).

Local streets: traffic is derived from the buildings served; motorcycles 30%, a value taken from Vietnam and Thailand because the WHO profile for Cambodia has no usable breakdown.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, the medium and heavy vehicles that counted roads of the same class carry; motorcycles 30 % of a town street's traffic (a local estimate); electric cars 0.5 % of cars, rolling noise only (IEA 2024).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 40 km/h in towns, 60 outside.

## Railways

Royal Railway publishes no timetable feed. Both lines out of Phnom Penh use the [world railway defaults](/about/methodology).

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, no wind farms.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Other industrial areas: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.

## Ships

[Global Fishing Watch](https://globalfishingwatch.org/our-apis/) AIS data, which has no class for yachts and pleasure boats.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 29 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Mosques call five times a day. Open-fronted bars and restaurants, their terraces open as long as they are.
