# South Korea

[Asia](/about/asia)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road keeps a fixed estimate per road class and lane.
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.5 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 2.3 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 60 outside, an expressway 100 (84 on a two-way one), a motorway 100; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Japan.
- **Railways:** No timetables: a fixed estimate per kind of line.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 86 air conditioners; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
