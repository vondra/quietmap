# Thailand

[Asia](/about/asia)

- **Roads:** Counted by the Department of Rural Roads' counts of 2024 on 1.4 % of the main roads by length and 39,497 km of smaller roads. Of the main roads, 99 % keep a fixed estimate per road class and lane.  National highways carry the Department of Highways' vehicle-kilometres per province.
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 39 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 0.8 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; without a speed sign a primary road 60 km/h in towns, 80 outside, a motorway 120; the day, evening and night shares measured here (the roadside monitors, 2025-26).
- **Railways:** Timetables on 1,943 of 6,594 km of railway; the lines without one a fixed estimate per kind of line.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 29 air conditioners (the region's median); mosques call five times a day; open-fronted bars and restaurants, their terraces open as long as they are.
