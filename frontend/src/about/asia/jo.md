## Roads

No open traffic counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world estimate per lane; smaller roads carry the trips their buildings make, else class defaults ([world defaults](/about/methodology)).

Local streets: traffic is derived from the buildings served; motorcycles 1% (floor), from 0.4 × the two-wheeler share of registered vehicles (WHO 2023 country profile, 2021 fleet).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, the medium and heavy vehicles that counted roads of the same class carry; motorcycles 0.1 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 0.8 % of cars, rolling noise only (IEA 2024).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns and outside.

## Railways

No timetable is loaded. All track mapped in OpenStreetMap uses the [world railway defaults](/about/methodology).

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, no wind farms.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Other industrial areas: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (a world value). Mosques call five times a day.
