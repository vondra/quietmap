# Timor-Leste

[Asia](/about/asia)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road keeps a fixed estimate per road class and lane.
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 60 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); without a speed sign a primary road 40 km/h in towns, 60 outside, a motorway 120; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Thailand.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 29 air conditioners (the region's median); mosques call five times a day; open-fronted bars and restaurants, their terraces open as long as they are.
