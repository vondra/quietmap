# Chile

[South America](/about/south-america)

- **Roads:** Counted by MOP's national census of 2025 on 3.3 % of the main roads by length. Of the main roads, 9.9 % follow what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads); 87 % keep a fixed estimate per road class and lane.
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 0.7 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 0.2 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 70 outside.
- **Railways:** No timetables: a fixed estimate per kind of line.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 16 air conditioners (the region's median); Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
