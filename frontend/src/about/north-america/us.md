# United States

[North America](/about/north-america)

- **Roads:** Counted by FHWA's Highway Performance Monitoring System of 2022 on 59 % of the main roads by length and 26,972 km of smaller roads. Of the main roads, 40 % follow what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).  Motorcycles, lorries and buses as FHWA's table VM-4 shares them per state, road group and area.
- **Traffic:** Electric cars 1.9 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; lorries and buses at most 105 km/h; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns and outside; the day, evening and night shares measured here (FHWA's counters, 2025).
- **Railways:** Timetables on 25,671 of 375,811 km of railway; the lines without one a fixed estimate per kind of line; horns at the level crossings of the FRA inventory.
- **Ground:** Terrain from USGS's 3DEP (the 48 states and Alaska); noise barriers from OpenStreetMap and the inventories of Washington, Florida and Virginia.
- **Wind turbines:** OpenStreetMap's standing turbines with USWTDB's power and height; 1,610 added that OpenStreetMap lacks.
- **Homes and places:** Heat pumps and air conditioners as the RECS survey counts them; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret; bars without mapped hours close at 02:00.
- **Checked against:** Minneapolis–St Paul airport's monitors (39).
