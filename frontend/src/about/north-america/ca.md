# Canada

[North America](/about/north-america)

- **Roads:** Counted by Québec's MTQ counts of 2024 on 12 % of the main roads by length and 8,307 km of smaller roads. Of the main roads, 88 % keep a fixed estimate per road class and lane.
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 0.6 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 2.4 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; lorries and buses at most 105 km/h; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns and outside; the day, evening and night shares as measured in the United States.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 8,436 of 62,983 km of railway; the lines without one a fixed estimate per kind of line; horns at the level crossings of Transport Canada's inventory.
- **Ground:** Terrain from MRDEM-30 (Natural Resources Canada).
- **Wind turbines:** OpenStreetMap's standing turbines with Natural Resources Canada's database's power and height; 110 added that OpenStreetMap lacks, 8 it calls dismantled removed.
- **Homes and places:** Heat pumps and air conditioners as Natural Resources Canada counts them; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret; bars without mapped hours close at 02:00.
