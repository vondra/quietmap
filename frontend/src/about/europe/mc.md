# Monaco

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road follows what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.5 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median); without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns and outside; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Germany, Britain and the Netherlands.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 3 of 3 km of railway; the lines without one a fixed estimate per kind of line.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 7 heat pumps and 11 air conditioners (the region's median); church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
