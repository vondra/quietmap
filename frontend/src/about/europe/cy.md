# Cyprus

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road keeps a fixed estimate per road class and lane.
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median); without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 65 outside, a motorway 100.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 16 air conditioners (a world value); Orthodox churches, and every church of no mapped denomination, ring before Saturday's and Sunday's services; the others three times a day and a Sunday peal; five calls a day and Friday's sala; bars without mapped hours open to 02:00 (03:00 at weekends), dinner 20-24 h.
