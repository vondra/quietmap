# Sweden

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** Counted by Trafikverket's road database (NVDB) of 2026 on 85 % of the main roads by length and 80,446 km of smaller roads; the EU city traffic volumes of 2023 on 198 km of smaller roads. Of the main roads, 13 % follow what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 7.5 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 70 outside, a motorway 110; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Germany, Britain and the Netherlands.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 9,281 of 17,049 km of railway; the lines without one carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight drawn to the TEN-T corridors; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Ground:** Terrain from Lantmäteriet's laser survey.
- **Wind turbines:** OpenStreetMap's standing turbines with Vindbrukskollen's power and height; 393 added that OpenStreetMap lacks, 57 it calls dismantled removed.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 38 heat pumps and 4 air conditioners (the region's median); church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
