# Germany

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** Counted by BASt's census of federal roads of 2021 on 50 % of the main roads by length and 1,009 km of smaller roads; BASt's census of motorways of 2021 on 33 % of the main roads by length. Of the main roads, 17 % follow what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.4 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 3.4 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 100 outside (traffic drives 84 on a two-way one), a motorway 130; the day, evening and night shares measured here (Baden-Württemberg's counters, 2025, lorries apart).
- **Railways:** Timetables on 46,710 of 82,576 km of railway; the lines without one carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight drawn to the TEN-T corridors; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Ground:** Terrain from the Länder's laser surveys (DGM1, DGM5); building heights measured in North Rhine-Westphalia (LoD1).
- **Wind turbines:** OpenStreetMap's standing turbines with the Marktstammdatenregister's power and height; 1,265 added that OpenStreetMap lacks, 523 it calls dismantled removed.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 4 heat pumps and 6 air conditioners; church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal, clocks striking the quarters by day and in the evening; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
- **Checked against:** The Eisenbahn-Bundesamt's railway monitors (19).
