# Austria

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road follows what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 2.7 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 3.9 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 100 outside (traffic drives 84 on a two-way one), a motorway 130; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Germany, Britain and the Netherlands.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 6,106 of 12,231 km of railway; the lines without one carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight drawn to the TEN-T corridors; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Ground:** Terrain from Austria's terrain model (DGM, 10 m).
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 9 heat pumps and 8 air conditioners; church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
