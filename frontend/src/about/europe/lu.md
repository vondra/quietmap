## Roads

Luxembourg City: [EU city traffic dataset](https://github.com/XavB64/traffic-volume-data-EU-cities).

Elsewhere motorways, trunk and primary roads follow one model of the trips made around them, fitted on counted roads; smaller roads carry the trips their buildings make ([method](/about/methodology)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, the medium and heavy vehicles that counted roads of the same class carry; motorcycles 2.2 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (traffic drives 76 on a two-way one), a motorway 130.

## Railways

Train and tram counts: [national timetable](https://data.public.lu/en/datasets/horaires-et-arrets-des-transport-publics-gtfs/) (CFL trains, Luxtram), one busy Wednesday. The timetable is passenger-only. Where no count exists, the class defaults are scaled to the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight comes from them, drawn to the TEN-T corridors, 37 % of it at night as at Germany's railway monitors.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 7 heat pumps and 11 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
