# Luxembourg

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** Counted by the EU city traffic volumes of 2023 on 3.7 % of the main roads by length. Of the main roads, 92 % follow what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 2.2 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (traffic drives 76 on a two-way one), a motorway 130; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Germany, Britain and the Netherlands.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 402 of 787 km of railway; the lines without one carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight drawn to the TEN-T corridors; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 7 heat pumps and 11 air conditioners (the region's median); church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
