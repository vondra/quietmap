## Roads

No traffic counts are loaded. Motorways, trunk and primary roads without a count follow one model of the trips made within 1, 5 and 15 km, fitted on counted roads; smaller roads carry the trips their buildings make ([method](/about/methodology)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, the medium and heavy vehicles that counted roads of the same class carry; motorcycles 0.8 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (traffic drives 76 on a two-way one), a motorway 130.

## Railways

Train counts: MÁV timetable from a [community mirror](https://gtfs.menetbrand.com/download/mav/), one busy Wednesday. Where no count exists, the class defaults are scaled to the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight comes from them, drawn to the TEN-T corridors, 37 % of it at night as at Germany's railway monitors.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 4 heat pumps (the region's median) and 28 air conditioners per 100 homes. Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
