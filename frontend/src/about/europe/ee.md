# Estonia

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road follows what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median); without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (traffic drives 76 on a two-way one); the day, evening and night shares as measured in Germany, Britain and the Netherlands.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 801 of 1,961 km of railway; the lines without one carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight drawn to the TEN-T corridors; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 32 heat pumps and 4 air conditioners (the region's median); church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
