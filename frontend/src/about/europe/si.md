# Slovenia

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** Counted by DRSI's traffic loads of 2024 on 7.3 % of the main roads by length and 302 km of smaller roads. Of the main roads, 68 % follow what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.5 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (traffic drives 76 on a two-way one), a motorway 130; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Spain.
- **Railways:** No timetable: the lines carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight drawn to the TEN-T corridors; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 9 heat pumps (the region's median) and 44 air conditioners; church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
