# Malta

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road follows what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median); without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 60 outside; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Spain.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 9 heat pumps (the region's median) and 84 air conditioners; church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret; bars without mapped hours open to 02:00 (03:00 at weekends), dinner 20-24 h.
