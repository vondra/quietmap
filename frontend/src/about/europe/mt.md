## Roads

No traffic counts are loaded. Motorways, trunk and primary roads without a count follow one model of the trips made within 1, 5 and 15 km, fitted on counted roads; smaller roads carry the trips their buildings make ([method](/about/methodology)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, the medium and heavy vehicles that counted roads of the same class carry; motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 60 outside.

## Railways

Malta has no railway.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 9 heat pumps (the region's median) and 84 air conditioners per 100 homes. Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays. Bars without mapped hours open to 02:00 (03:00 at weekends), dinner 20-24 h.
