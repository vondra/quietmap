# Czech Republic

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** Counted by ŘSD's national traffic census of 2020 on 84 % of the main roads by length and 22,823 km of smaller roads; Prague's street counts (TSK) of 2025 on 2.4 % of the main roads by length and 467 km of smaller roads; Brno's detectors of 2023 on 1.1 % of the main roads by length and 256 km of smaller roads. Of the main roads, 12 % follow what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 4.2 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (traffic drives 76 on a two-way one), an expressway 110 (93 on a two-way one), a motorway 130; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Germany, Britain and the Netherlands.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 11,047 of 19,320 km of railway; the lines without one carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight drawn to the TEN-T corridors; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Ground:** Terrain from ČÚZK's laser survey DMR 5G; building heights measured in Prague (IPR Praha) and from the RÚIAN register's floor counts for 876,753 buildings.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 6 heat pumps and 16 air conditioners; church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
- **Checked against:** Prague airport's monitors (14).
