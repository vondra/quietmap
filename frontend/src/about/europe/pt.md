# Portugal

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road follows what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.3 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 2.3 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 80 outside, a motorway 120; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Spain.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 2,844 of 4,057 km of railway; the lines without one carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight drawn to the TEN-T corridors; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Ground:** Terrain from DGT's laser survey (2 m).
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 8 heat pumps and 17 air conditioners; church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret; bars without mapped hours open to 02:00 (03:00 at weekends), dinner 20-24 h.
