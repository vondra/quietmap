# Spain

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** Counted by MITMA's traffic map of 2022 on 46 % of the main roads by length and 947 km of smaller roads; the EU city traffic volumes of 2023 on 400 km of smaller roads. Of the main roads, 54 % follow what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 3 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 0.8 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; lorries and buses at most 90 km/h; without a speed sign a primary road 30 km/h in towns, 80 outside, a motorway 120; the day, evening and night shares measured here (Madrid's and Barcelona's monitors, 2025).
- **Railways:** Timetables on 15,108 of 24,554 km of railway; the lines without one carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight drawn to the TEN-T corridors; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Ground:** Terrain from IGN's MDT05 from the PNOA laser survey; building heights from the Catastro's floor counts for 3,514,326 buildings.
- **Wind turbines:** OpenStreetMap's standing turbines with Castilla-La Mancha's register's power and height; 53 added that OpenStreetMap lacks.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 9 heat pumps and 41 air conditioners; church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret; bars without mapped hours open to 02:00 (03:00 at weekends), dinner 20-24 h.
- **Checked against:** Barcelona's network (154); Madrid's network (31).
