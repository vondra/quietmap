# Norway

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** Counted by Statens vegvesen's road database (NVDB) of 2025 on 16 % of the main roads by length and 3,295 km of smaller roads. Of the main roads, 75 % follow what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median); electric cars 27.6 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 80 outside (traffic drives 68 on a two-way one), a motorway 110; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Germany, Britain and the Netherlands.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 4,102 of 5,273 km of railway; the lines without one carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Wind turbines:** OpenStreetMap's standing turbines with NVE's register's power and height; 3 added that OpenStreetMap lacks.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 60 heat pumps and 4 air conditioners (the region's median); church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
- **Checked against:** Silenzi in Quota's quiet mountains (10).
