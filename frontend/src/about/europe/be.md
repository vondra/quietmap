## Roads

No traffic counts are loaded. Motorways, trunk and primary roads without a count follow one model of the trips made within 1, 5 and 15 km, fitted on counted roads; smaller roads carry the trips their buildings make ([method](/about/methodology)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, the medium and heavy vehicles that counted roads of the same class carry; motorcycles 1.2 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 4.1 % of cars, rolling noise only (IEA 2024); lorries and buses at most 90 km/h.

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns and outside.

## Railways

Train counts, one busy Wednesday:

- National railway: [SNCB/NMBS](https://gtfs.irail.be/).
- Trams, metro and light rail: [STIB/MIVB](https://stibmivb.opendatasoft.com/) (Brussels), [De Lijn](http://gtfs.irail.be/de-lijn/de_lijn-gtfs.zip) (Antwerp, Ghent, coast tram), [TEC](http://opendata.tec-wl.be/Current%20GTFS/TEC-GTFS.zip) (Charleroi).

No Belgian timetable includes it. Where no count exists, the class defaults are scaled to the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight comes from them, drawn to the TEN-T corridors, 37 % of it at night as at Germany's railway monitors.

## Buildings and terrain

Terrain from Flanders' DHMV II and Wallonia's laser survey.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 6 heat pumps and 12 air conditioners per 100 homes. Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
