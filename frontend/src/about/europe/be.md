# Belgium

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road follows what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.2 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 4.1 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; lorries and buses at most 90 km/h; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns and outside; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Germany, Britain and the Netherlands.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 5,222 of 9,559 km of railway; the lines without one carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight drawn to the TEN-T corridors; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Ground:** Terrain from Flanders' DHMV II and Wallonia's laser survey.
- **Wind turbines:** OpenStreetMap's standing turbines with RIVM's register's power and height.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 6 heat pumps and 12 air conditioners; church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
