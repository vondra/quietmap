# Ireland

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** Counted by TII's counters of 2020 on 88 % of the main roads by length and 514 km of smaller roads; the EU city traffic volumes of 2023 on 119 km of smaller roads. Of the main roads, 12 % follow what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median); lorries and buses at most 90 km/h; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 80 outside (traffic drives 68 on a two-way one), a motorway 120; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Germany, Britain and the Netherlands.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 1,982 of 2,300 km of railway; the lines without one carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight drawn to the TEN-T corridors; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Ground:** Terrain from Geological Survey Ireland's laser survey, where it flew.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 7 heat pumps and 4 air conditioners (the region's median); church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret; pubs without mapped hours close at 23:30 (00:30 at weekends).
- **Checked against:** Dublin's network (14).
