## Roads

National roads: [Transport Infrastructure Ireland](https://data.tii.ie/) counters. Daily traffic is that of one ordinary pre-pandemic weekday, 19 June 2019, in the vehicle classes the counters report. Newer daily files are not openly downloadable.

Dublin also has counts from the [EU city traffic dataset](https://github.com/XavB64/traffic-volume-data-EU-cities). Regional and local roads have no counts. Uncounted streets carry the trips their buildings make. Uncounted main roads follow one model of the trips made around them, fitted on counted roads.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, the medium and heavy vehicles that counted roads of the same class carry; motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median); lorries and buses at most 90 km/h.

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 80 outside (traffic drives 68 on a two-way one), a motorway 120.

## Railways

Train, DART and Luas counts: [Transport for Ireland timetable](https://www.transportforireland.ie/transitData/Data/GTFS_All.zip), one busy Wednesday. The timetable is passenger-only. Where no count exists, the class defaults are scaled to the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight comes from them, drawn to the TEN-T corridors, 37 % of it at night as at Germany's railway monitors.

## Buildings and terrain

Ireland has open building outlines but no open heights or floor counts. Heights: OpenStreetMap tags and the typical height for the footprint size.

Terrain from Geological Survey Ireland's laser survey, where it flew.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 7 heat pumps and 4 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays. Pubs without mapped hours close at 23:30 (00:30 at weekends).
