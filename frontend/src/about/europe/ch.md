# Switzerland

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** Counted by ASTRA's counts of 2024 on 2.1 % of the main roads by length. Of the main roads, 86 % follow what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 2.3 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 4.6 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 80 outside (traffic drives 68 on a two-way one), a motorway 120; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Germany, Britain and the Netherlands.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 5,992 of 9,695 km of railway; the lines without one a fixed estimate per kind of line; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Ground:** Terrain from swissALTI3D.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 7 heat pumps (the region's median) and 10 air conditioners; church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal, clocks striking the quarters day and night; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
- **Checked against:** Zurich airport's monitors (4).
