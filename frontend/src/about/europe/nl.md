# Netherlands

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** Counted by Rijkswaterstaat's motorway counts (INWEVA) of 2024 on 32 % of the main roads by length. Estimated per road class from Amsterdam's traffic model of 2025 on 539 km of smaller roads. Of the main roads, 65 % follow what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median); electric cars 6.0 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 80 outside (traffic drives 68 on a two-way one), an expressway 100 (84 on a two-way one), a motorway 130; the day, evening and night shares measured here on motorways (INWEVA 2024).
- **Railways:** Timetables on 4,965 of 7,578 km of railway; the lines without one carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight drawn to the TEN-T corridors; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Ground:** Terrain from AHN4 (laser survey); building heights measured for every building (3DBAG); noise barriers from OpenStreetMap and Rijkswaterstaat's inventory.
- **Wind turbines:** OpenStreetMap's standing turbines with RIVM's register's power and height.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 7 heat pumps and 12 air conditioners; church bells three times a day and a Sunday peal; Friday's noon call only, from a mapped minaret.
