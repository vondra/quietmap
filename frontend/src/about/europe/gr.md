# Greece

[Europe](/about/europe)

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Every main road follows what the buildings within 1, 5 and 15 km make (one model fitted on counted roads).
- **Traffic:** Motorcycles 3.9 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 0.4 % of cars (IEA 2024), rolling noise only; without a speed sign a primary road 50 km/h in towns and outside, a motorway 130; the day, evening and night shares as measured in Spain.
- **Railways:** Timetables on 967 of 3,248 km of railway; the lines without one carry the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight drawn to the TEN-T corridors; freight without a count 37 % at night, as Germany's monitors count it.
- **Homes and places:** Per 100 of the homes OpenStreetMap maps: 9 heat pumps (the region's median) and 76 air conditioners; Orthodox churches, and every church of no mapped denomination, ring before Saturday's and Sunday's services; the others three times a day and a Sunday peal; mosques call five times a day; bars without mapped hours open to 02:00 (03:00 at weekends), dinner 20-24 h.
