## Roads

Traffic volumes: no national counts. Egypt-specific estimates by OpenStreetMap class, scaled ×2.5 in Greater Cairo and Alexandria and ×1.5 in 19 other cities: governorate capitals, the Suez Canal cities and the Red Sea resorts.

| Road class | Open country | Cairo and Alexandria (×2.5) | Other listed cities (×1.5) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Motorway | 40,000 | 100,000 | 60,000 |
| Trunk | 15,000 | 37,500 | 22,500 |
| Primary | 7,000 | 17,500 | 10,500 |
| Secondary | 3,500 | 8,750 | 5,250 |
| Tertiary | 1,500 | 3,750 | 2,250 |
| Residential | 700 | 1,750 | 1,050 |

Vehicles per day, both directions. Slip roads take half the value of their road. Service roads, tracks and unclassified roads keep the world default.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, the medium and heavy vehicles that counted roads of the same class carry; motorcycles 13 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 60 km/h in towns, 90 outside (traffic drives 76 on a two-way one).

## Industry

Operating power plants: Global Energy Monitor, matched to OpenStreetMap industrial areas; plants without a mapped area are absent. Other sites are classified from their OpenStreetMap name, otherwise as generic industry.

## Ships

AIS vessel hours: EMODnet 2024 where its European grid reaches this coast, Global Fishing Watch elsewhere.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (a world value). Mosques: five calls a day and the iqama.
