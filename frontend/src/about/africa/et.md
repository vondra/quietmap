Traffic volumes: no national counts. Ethiopia-specific estimates by OpenStreetMap class, scaled ×2 in Addis Ababa and ×1.4 in 18 regional capitals and larger towns.

Vehicles per day, both directions. Slip roads take half the value of their road. Service roads, tracks and unclassified roads keep the world default.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, the medium and heavy vehicles that counted roads of the same class carry; motorcycles 3 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns and outside.
