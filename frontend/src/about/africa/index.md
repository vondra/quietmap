# Africa

What the map knows in Africa, country by country below. Main roads are the motorways, trunk and primary roads of OpenStreetMap.

- **Roads:** No open traffic counts. Uncounted streets carry their buildings' trips; uncounted main roads keep a fixed estimate per road class.
- **Railways:** No timetables yet.
- **Terrain:** The GEDTM30 world model (30 m).
