## Roads

Traffic volumes: no national counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world estimate per lane; secondary and tertiary roads follow a model of the trips made within 5 km, fitted on European counts, and streets carry the trips their buildings make. Where too few buildings are mapped, roads keep the class defaults ([world defaults](/about/data-preparation)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 0.2 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (cars drive 76 on a two-way one), a motorway 110.

## Railways

Train counts: no timetable. Mapped active railways use the [world railway defaults](/about/data-preparation). Trams and light rail take 120 and 80 trains per day. These values may be close for the Tunis light rail, the TGM and the Tunis to Sousse main line. On the phosphate lines around Gafsa and the interior branches the passenger count is too high and the freight volume is unknown.

## Industry

Operating power plants: Global Energy Monitor, matched to OpenStreetMap industrial areas; plants without a mapped area are absent. Other sites are classified from their OpenStreetMap name, otherwise as generic industry.

## Ships

AIS vessel hours: EMODnet 2024 where its European grid reaches this coast, Global Fishing Watch elsewhere.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (a world value). Mosques call five times a day.
