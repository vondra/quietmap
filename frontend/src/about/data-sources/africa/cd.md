## Roads

Traffic follows the [global road model](/about/data-preparation): road class and lanes on main roads, and the trips generated by mapped buildings on smaller roads. These are estimates; no local count verifies them. The source list below states the count coverage for each map year.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 1.5 % of a town street's traffic (the world median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns and outside, a motorway 120.


## Railways

Train counts: no timetable. Per-line estimates based on services reported by SNCC; a track takes the first matching row.

| Line | Passenger trains per day | Freight trains per day |

| Matadi to Kinshasa | 2 | 4 |

| Kamina to Ilebo | 1 | 1 |

A line matches within 8 km of its manually drawn route.

## Industry

Operating power plants: Global Energy Monitor, matched to OpenStreetMap industrial areas; plants without a mapped area are absent. Other sites are classified from their OpenStreetMap name, otherwise as generic industry.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 6 air conditioners per 100 homes (South Africa's survey). Mosques call five times a day.
