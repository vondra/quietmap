## Roads

No open traffic counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world estimate per lane; secondary and tertiary roads follow a model of the trips made within 5 km, fitted on European counts ([world defaults](/about/data-preparation)).

Where buildings are mapped, residential, living, service and unclassified streets carry the trips their buildings make; where too few are mapped, roads keep the class defaults.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 3.4 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns and outside.

## Railways

No public railway. The sugar mills' cane tramways take the defaults year-round, with no seasonal adjustment: 15 freight trains a day on the 88 km that OpenStreetMap maps as industrial railway, 10 trains a day on track mapped as narrow gauge.

## Industry

Power plants: Global Energy Monitor, operating plants only, where OSM maps an industrial area at the site. Other industry: OSM industrial areas with a type inferred from name and tags.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (a world value). Mosques call five times a day.
