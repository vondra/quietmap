## Roads

No traffic counts are loaded. Motorways, trunk and primary roads follow one model of the trips made within 1, 5 and 15 km, fitted on counted roads, on the 2026 map (class defaults on the 2024 map), and secondary and tertiary roads a fit of the trips made within 5 km. Streets carry the trips their buildings make ([method](/about/data-preparation)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 0.8 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (cars drive 76 on a two-way one), a motorway 130.

## Railways

Train counts: on the 2026 map, MÁV timetable from a [community mirror](https://gtfs.menetbrand.com/download/mav/), one busy Wednesday; because no 2024 Hungarian archive is available, the 2026 MÁV timetable stands as a declared fallback vintage (+2 years) on the 2024 map, averaged over its own service calendar alongside cross-border 2024 timetables, with remaining uncovered lines lowered toward Hungary's latest published Eurostat passenger train-kilometres (2014). Lines without a count take the class defaults; freight comes from them, scaled to the country's goods train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024), drawn to the TEN-T corridors, with the [European night share](/about/data-sources/europe#railways).

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 4 heat pumps (the region's median) and 28 air conditioners per 100 homes. Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
