## Roads

Vienna: the city's [permanent counting stations](https://www.data.gv.at/katalog/dataset/stadt-wien_verkehrszhlstellenzhlwertewien) and the [EU city traffic dataset](https://github.com/XavB64/traffic-volume-data-EU-cities).

Elsewhere motorways, trunk and primary roads follow one model of the trips made around them, fitted on counted roads, and secondary and tertiary roads a fit of the trips made within 5 km; streets carry the trips their buildings make ([method](/about/data-preparation)). ASFINAG motorway counts are not loaded yet.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 2.7 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 3.9 % of cars, rolling noise only (IEA 2024).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 100 outside (cars drive 84 on a two-way one), a motorway 130.

## Railways

Train and tram counts: [ÖBB timetable](https://data.oebb.at/de/datensaetze~soll-fahrplan-gtfs~), one busy Wednesday. The timetable is passenger-only. Lines without a count take the class defaults; freight comes from them, scaled to the country's goods train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024), drawn to the TEN-T corridors, 37 % of it at night as at Germany's railway monitors.

## Buildings and terrain

Terrain from Austria's terrain model ([DGM](https://www.data.gv.at/katalog/dataset/dgm), 10 m; geoland.at, CC BY 4.0).

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 9 heat pumps and 8 air conditioners per 100 homes. Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
