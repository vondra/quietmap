## Roads

Motorways and main roads: the [2024 annual results](https://www.astra.admin.ch/de/schweizerische-automatische-strassenverkehrszaehlung-sasvz) of the federal SASVZ counting stations (open use with attribution: ASTRA), with the yearly average, heavy traffic and heavy goods totals per station.

A road takes the nearest station on a compatible class within 200 m; coaches form the medium class and 1 % of the station total is assigned to motorcycles. Stations without a usable 2024 year are skipped.

Zurich and Geneva: [EU city traffic dataset](https://github.com/XavB64/traffic-volume-data-EU-cities).

Uncounted streets carry the trips their buildings make. Uncounted motorways, trunk and primary roads follow one model of the trips made around them, fitted on counted roads, and secondary and tertiary roads a fit of the trips made within 5 km.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 2.3 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 4.6 % of cars, rolling noise only (IEA 2024).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 80 outside (cars drive 68 on a two-way one), a motorway 120.

## Railways

Train and tram counts: national timetable of [opentransportdata.swiss](https://opentransportdata.swiss/) (SBB and the regional operators), one busy Wednesday. The timetable is passenger-only; freight comes from the class defaults, 31.4 trains a day on a main line (fitted to the national goods train-kilometres of 2023), 37 % of it at night as at Germany's railway monitors.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 7 heat pumps (the region's median) and 10 air conditioners per 100 homes. Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal; church clocks strike the quarters day and night. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
