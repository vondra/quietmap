## Roads

National roads and 476 km of regional road: [Transport Infrastructure Ireland](https://data.tii.ie/) counters, each road taking a counter of its own number up to 25 km away. Daily traffic is that of one ordinary pre-pandemic weekday, 19 June 2019, in the vehicle classes the counters report. Newer daily files are not openly downloadable.

Dublin also has counts from the [EU city traffic dataset](https://github.com/XavB64/traffic-volume-data-EU-cities). Other regional roads and local roads have no counts. Uncounted streets carry the trips their buildings make. Uncounted motorways, trunk and primary roads follow one model of the trips made around them, fitted on counted roads, and secondary and tertiary roads a fit of the trips made within 5 km.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles the share counted on nearby roads, else 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median); lorries and buses at most 90 km/h.

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 80 outside (cars drive 68 on a two-way one), a motorway 120.

## Railways

Train, DART and Luas counts: [Transport for Ireland timetable](https://www.transportforireland.ie/transitData/Data/GTFS_All.zip), one busy Wednesday. The timetable is passenger-only. Lines without a count take the class defaults; freight comes from them, scaled to the country's goods train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024), drawn to the TEN-T corridors, 37 % of it at night as at Germany's railway monitors.

## Buildings and terrain

Ireland has open building outlines but no open heights or floor counts. Heights: OpenStreetMap tags and the typical height for the footprint size.

Terrain from Geological Survey Ireland's [laser survey](https://gsi.geodata.gov.ie/) (CC BY 4.0), where it flew.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 7 heat pumps and 4 air conditioners (the region's median) per 100 homes. Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays. Pubs without mapped hours close at 23:30 (00:30 at weekends).
