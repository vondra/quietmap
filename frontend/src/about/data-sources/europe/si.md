## Roads

Motorways, expressways, main and regional roads: the [2024 traffic load map](https://podatki.gov.si/dataset/pldp-karte-prometnih-obremenitev) of DRSI (CC BY 4.0), with motorcycles, cars, buses, vans and four truck classes per counting site.

A road takes the nearest counting site on a compatible class within 200 m. Sections whose traffic DRSI estimated rather than counted are skipped, and short-term counts keep their total with the class split marked estimated.

Uncounted streets carry the trips their buildings make. Uncounted motorways, trunk and primary roads follow one model of the trips made around them, fitted on counted roads, and secondary and tertiary roads a fit of the trips made within 5 km.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles the share counted on nearby roads, else 1.5 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (cars drive 76 on a two-way one), a motorway 130.

## Railways

No timetable is loaded. Class defaults apply: 80 passenger and 20 freight trains per day on main lines, 30 and 5 on branch lines, 120 trams on tram tracks.

Where no count exists, the class defaults are scaled to the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight comes from them, drawn to the TEN-T corridors, 37 % of it at night as at Germany's railway monitors.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 9 heat pumps (the region's median) and 44 air conditioners per 100 homes. Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
