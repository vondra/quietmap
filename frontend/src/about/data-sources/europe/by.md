## Roads

No traffic counts are loaded. Motorways, trunk and primary roads follow one model of the trips made within 1, 5 and 15 km, fitted on counted roads, and secondary and tertiary roads a fit of the trips made within 5 km. Streets carry the trips their buildings make ([method](/about/data-preparation)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 60 km/h in towns, 90 outside (cars drive 76 on a two-way one), a motorway 110.

## Railways

No timetable is loaded. Class defaults apply: 80 passenger and 20 freight trains per day on main lines, 30 and 5 on branch lines, 120 trams on tram tracks. The Minsk metro is underground and emits no noise.

## Industry

Power plants: [Global Energy Monitor](https://globalenergymonitor.org/) power tracker, operating plants with a site drawn in OpenStreetMap; wind farms are skipped. Other industrial sites are OpenStreetMap areas with a level estimated from tag or name.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 4 heat pumps and 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Orthodox churches, and every church of no mapped denomination, ring before Saturday's and Sunday's services, the others three times a day and a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
