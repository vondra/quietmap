All values describe Ukraine before February 2022. No data is available on traffic near the front or in occupied areas.

## Roads

Traffic follows the [global road model](/about/data-preparation): road class and lanes on main roads, and the trips generated by mapped buildings on smaller roads. These are estimates; no local count verifies them. The source list below states the count coverage for each map year.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (cars drive 76 on a two-way one), a motorway 130.

## Industry

Power plants: [Global Energy Monitor](https://globalenergymonitor.org/) power tracker, operating plants only. The list has not been checked against war damage since 2022.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 4 heat pumps and 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Orthodox churches, and every church of no mapped denomination, ring before Saturday's and Sunday's services, the others three times a day and a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
