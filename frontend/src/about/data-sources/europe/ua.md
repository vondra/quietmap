All Ukraine-specific road (2026 map) and railway values describe Ukraine before February 2022. No data is available on traffic near the front or in occupied areas.

Streets carry the trips their buildings make.

On the 2024 map, these pre-2022 country tables and city multipliers are not used: traffic follows the [global road model](/about/data-preparation) (world class defaults on main roads, and the trips generated by mapped buildings on smaller roads).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (cars drive 76 on a two-way one), a motorway 130.
