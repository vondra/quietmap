## Roads

No traffic counts are loaded. Motorways, trunk and primary roads follow one model of the trips made within 1, 5 and 15 km, fitted on counted roads, and secondary and tertiary roads a fit of the trips made within 5 km. Streets carry the trips their buildings make ([method](/about/data-preparation)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 0.8 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 9.7 % of cars, rolling noise only (IEA 2024).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 80 outside (cars drive 68 on a two-way one).

## Railways

Iceland has no railway.

## Industry

Power plants: [Global Energy Monitor](https://globalenergymonitor.org/) power tracker, operating plants with a site drawn in OpenStreetMap; wind farms are skipped. Other industrial sites are OpenStreetMap areas with a level estimated from tag or name.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 32 heat pumps and 4 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
