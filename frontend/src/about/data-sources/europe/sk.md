## Roads

No traffic counts are loaded. Motorways, trunk and primary roads follow one model of the trips made within 1, 5 and 15 km, fitted on counted roads, on the 2026 map (class defaults on the 2024 map), and secondary and tertiary roads a fit of the trips made within 5 km. Streets carry the trips their buildings make ([method](/about/data-preparation)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (cars drive 76 on a two-way one), a motorway 130.

## Railways

Train counts: ŽSR timetable on the [national open data portal](https://data.slovensko.sk/), one busy Wednesday on the 2026 map; on the 2024 map, the archived 2024 ŽSR timetable (75 of 366 days covered), averaged over 2024 with missing dates estimated from the covered-day mean. Lines without a count take the class defaults; freight comes from them, scaled to the country's goods train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024), drawn to the TEN-T corridors, with the [European night share](/about/data-sources/europe#railways).

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 4 heat pumps and 16 air conditioners (the region's median) per 100 homes. Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
