## Roads

No traffic counts are loaded. Motorways, trunk and primary roads follow one model of the trips made within 1, 5 and 15 km, fitted on counted roads, and secondary and tertiary roads a fit of the trips made within 5 km. Streets carry the trips their buildings make ([method](/about/data-preparation)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside (cars drive 76 on a two-way one).

## Railways

Train and tram counts: national [Peatus timetable](https://peatus.ee/) (Elron trains, Tallinn trams), one busy Wednesday. Where no count exists, the class defaults are scaled to the country's train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024); freight comes from them, drawn to the TEN-T corridors, 37 % of it at night as at Germany's railway monitors.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 32 heat pumps and 4 air conditioners (the region's median) per 100 homes. Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
