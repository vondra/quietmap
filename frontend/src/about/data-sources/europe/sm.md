## Roads

No traffic counts are loaded. Motorways, trunk and primary roads without a count follow one model of the trips made within 1, 5 and 15 km, fitted on counted roads; smaller roads carry the trips their buildings make ([method](/about/data-preparation)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, the medium and heavy vehicles that counted roads of the same class carry; motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns and outside.

## Railways

San Marino has no railway.

## Industry

Power plants: [Global Energy Monitor](https://globalenergymonitor.org/) power tracker, operating plants with a site drawn in OpenStreetMap; wind farms are skipped. Other industrial sites are OpenStreetMap areas with a level estimated from tag or name.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 9 heat pumps and 55 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
