## Roads

No traffic counts are loaded. Motorways, trunk and primary roads follow one model of the trips made within 1, 5 and 15 km, fitted on counted roads, and secondary and tertiary roads a fit of the trips made within 5 km. Streets carry the trips their buildings make ([method](/about/data-preparation)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns and outside, a motorway 130.

## Railways

Train counts: [HŽ Putnički prijevoz timetable](https://www.hzpp.hr/), one busy Wednesday. Lines without a count take the class defaults; freight comes from them, scaled to the country's goods train-kilometres (Eurostat 2024), drawn to the TEN-T corridors, 37 % of it at night as at Germany's railway monitors. Trams in Zagreb and Osijek have no feed loaded and default to 120 per day on every track.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 9 heat pumps (the region's median) and 55 air conditioners per 100 homes. Church bells ring three times a day, with a Sunday peal. Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
