## Roads

No traffic counts are loaded. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world estimate per lane. Where buildings are mapped, secondary and tertiary roads follow a fit of the trips made within 5 km and streets carry the trips their buildings make; elsewhere class defaults apply ([world defaults](/about/data-preparation)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 65 outside, a motorway 100.

## Railways

Cyprus has no railway.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (a world value). Orthodox churches, and every church of no mapped denomination, ring before Saturday's and Sunday's services, the others three times a day and a Sunday peal. Mosques: five calls a day and the sala on Friday by day and on Thursday evening. Bars without mapped hours open to 02:00 (03:00 at weekends), dinner 20-24 h.
