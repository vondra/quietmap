## Roads

No open traffic counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world estimate per lane; secondary and tertiary roads follow the trips made by the buildings within 5 km, streets the trips their buildings make, else class defaults ([world defaults](/about/data-preparation)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 56 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 60 km/h in towns and outside.

## Railways

Sri Lanka Railways publishes no timetable feed. All lines use the [world railway defaults](/about/data-preparation).

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, no wind farms.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Other industrial areas: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.

## Ships

[Global Fishing Watch](https://globalfishingwatch.org/our-apis/) AIS data, which has no class for yachts and pleasure boats.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Mosques call five times a day. Open-fronted bars and restaurants, their terraces open as long as they are.
