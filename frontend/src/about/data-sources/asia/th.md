## Roads

Rural roads: Department of Rural Roads daily traffic for 2024, per road and in twelve vehicle classes including motorcycles, from the [Ministry of Transport data portal](https://datagov.mot.go.th/). Counts are matched to OpenStreetMap by the exact Thai road code, such as นบ.3021; the published vehicle split is kept.

National highways: the Department of Highways' traffic table for 2021 gives the vehicle-kilometres a year of 2,695 control sections, in ten vehicle classes and motorcycles, each in its province. A highway's vehicle-kilometres in a province over the length of its OpenStreetMap roads there give its mean daily traffic across both directions, with the measured vehicle shares: highway 1 carries 124,800 vehicles a day in Pathum Thani and 11,900 in Chai Nat. These means cover 50,145 of the 53,510 km of roads with a department number and miss the department's 2025 counts at 61 stations in Nakhon Sawan by 1.3 dB on average.

Where their buildings are mapped, uncounted roads of the rural road network follow a fit of the network's counts on the trips made by the buildings within 5 km; other secondary and tertiary roads follow the same kind of fit made on 13 European countries, and streets carry the trips their buildings make.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles the share counted on rural roads nearby, else 39 % of a town street's traffic, 30 % of a secondary road's, 20 % of a trunk or primary road's and 4 % of a motorway's (WHO 2023); electric cars 0.8 % of cars, rolling noise only (IEA 2024).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 60 km/h in towns, 80 outside, a motorway 120.

## Railways

Train counts come from the national [Namtang timetable](https://namtang-api.otp.go.th/download/namtang-gtfs.zip) of the Office of Transport and Traffic Policy and Planning, busiest Wednesday. It covers the State Railway, the BTS Skytrain and the Airport Rail Link; freight is not in it and takes the class defaults.

Surface metro sections are included; see the [railway method](/about/data-preparation).

## Industry

- Power plants: [Global Power Plant Database](https://datasets.wri.org/dataset/globalpowerplantdatabase), last updated in 2021; newer plants are missing.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: Global Energy Monitor trackers.
- Other industrial areas, including Map Ta Phut and Laem Chabang: OSM polygons with a generic sound level. The Thai factory register needs a login and is not used.

## Ships

[Global Fishing Watch](https://globalfishingwatch.org/our-apis/) AIS data, which has no class for yachts and pleasure boats.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 29 air conditioners per 100 homes (South-East Asia's figure). Mosques call five times a day. Open-fronted bars and restaurants, their terraces open as long as they are.
