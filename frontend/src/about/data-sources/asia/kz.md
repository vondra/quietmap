## Roads

Traffic follows the [global road model](/about/data-preparation): road class and lanes on main roads, and the trips generated by mapped buildings on smaller roads. These are estimates; no local count verifies them. The source list below states the count coverage for each map year.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 1.1 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 60 km/h in towns, 100 outside, a motorway 140.


## Railways

No open timetable. Train counts are assigned per corridor:

| Track | Passenger | Freight |
|---|---:|---:|
| Within 20 km of the Pavlodar to Ekibastuz to Karaganda coal line | 2 | 30 |
| Within 18 km of the Almaty to Astana line | 8 | 20 |
| Other main lines | 2 | 10 |
| Branches | 1 | 4 |
| Industrial sidings | 0 | 6 |

Tram and light-rail track gets 80 trains per day in Almaty, 50 in Astana and 60 elsewhere. Surface metro sections are included; see the [railway method](/about/data-preparation).

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, wind farms excluded.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Oil fields and metal smelters not in those lists: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (a world value). Mosques call five times a day.
