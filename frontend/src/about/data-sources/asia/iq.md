## Roads

On the 2026 map: Iraq publishes no open traffic counts. Traffic is assigned by road class and city:

| Road class | Open country | 14 provincial cities (×1.3) | Basra, Erbil, Sulaymaniyah (×1.6) | Baghdad (×2.5) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Motorway | 40,000 | 52,000 | 64,000 | 100,000 |
| Trunk | 18,000 | 23,400 | 28,800 | 45,000 |
| Primary | 9,000 | 11,700 | 14,400 | 22,500 |
| Secondary | 4,500 | 5,850 | 7,200 | 11,250 |
| Tertiary | 2,000 | 2,600 | 3,200 | 5,000 |
| Residential | 800 | 1,040 | 1,280 | 2,000 |

Each city is a box around its centre, not its administrative boundary. Residential streets take the table's value only where their buildings are not mapped; elsewhere they carry the trips their buildings make.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 0.3 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

On the 2024 map, these network tables and manually drawn city boxes are not used: traffic follows the [global road model](/about/data-preparation) (world class defaults on main roads, and the trips generated by mapped buildings on smaller roads).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 60 km/h in towns, 80 outside (cars drive 68 on a two-way one).


## Railways

No timetable. Track within 12 km of the Baghdad to Basra line gets 3 passenger and 4 freight trains per day and all other track gets 1 and 1 (also applied as a declared fallback vintage, +2 years, on the 2024 map). Parts of the network are not in service.

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, wind farms excluded.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Oil fields and refineries: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (a world value). Mosques call five times a day.
