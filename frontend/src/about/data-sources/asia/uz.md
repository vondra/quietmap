## Roads

Traffic follows the [global road model](/about/data-preparation): road class and lanes on main roads, and the trips generated by mapped buildings on smaller roads. These are estimates; no local count verifies them. The source list below states the count coverage for each map year.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 1.5 % of a town street's traffic (the world median); electric cars 0.2 % of cars, rolling noise only (IEA 2024).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 60 km/h in towns, 70 outside.

| Tashkent to the Fergana Valley (within 12 km) | 5 | 11 |

| Samarkand to Termez (within 12 km) | 4 | 12 |
| Other main lines | 4 | 8 |
| Branches | 2 | 5 |

Tram and light-rail track gets 200 trains per day. Surface metro sections are included; see the [railway method](/about/data-preparation).

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, wind farms excluded.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Other factories: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (a world value). Mosques call five times a day.
