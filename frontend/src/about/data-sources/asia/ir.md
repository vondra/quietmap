## Roads

Traffic follows the [global road model](/about/data-preparation): road class and lanes on main roads, and the trips generated by mapped buildings on smaller roads. These are estimates; no local count verifies them. The source list below states the count coverage for each map year.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 0.3 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 60 km/h in towns, 90 outside (cars drive 76 on a two-way one).


## Railways

No open timetable. Counts are assigned for four corridors out of Tehran; all other track gets 3 passenger and 5 freight trains per day, industrial sidings 5 freight.

| Corridor (track within 9 km) | Passenger | Freight |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tehran to Mashhad | 15 | 8 |
| Tehran to Isfahan and Shiraz | 8 | 10 |
| Tehran to Tabriz | 5 | 8 |
| Tehran to Bandar Abbas | 3 | 10 |

Tram and light-rail track gets 350 trains per day in Tehran and 80 elsewhere. Surface metro sections are included; see the [railway method](/about/data-preparation).

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, wind farms excluded.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Refineries, petrochemical plants and steel works not in those lists: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.

## Ships

[Global Fishing Watch](https://globalfishingwatch.org/our-apis/) AIS data, which has no class for yachts and pleasure boats.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Mosques call five times a day.
