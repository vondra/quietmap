## Roads

No open traffic counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world estimate per lane; secondary and tertiary roads follow the trips made by the buildings within 5 km, streets the trips their buildings make, else class defaults ([world defaults](/about/data-preparation)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 72 % of a town street's traffic, 54 % of a secondary road's, 36 % of a trunk or primary road's and 7 % of a motorway's (WHO 2023); electric cars 4.4 % of cars, rolling noise only (IEA 2024).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 60 km/h in towns and outside, a motorway 120.

## Railways

Vietnam Railways and the metros in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City publish no timetable. All lines use the [world railway defaults](/about/data-preparation).

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor list for Vietnam, operating units only, wind farms excluded, matched to OpenStreetMap industrial areas within 1.5 km.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Other factories: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.

## Ships

[Global Fishing Watch](https://globalfishingwatch.org/our-apis/) AIS data, which has no class for yachts and pleasure boats.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 29 air conditioners per 100 homes (South-East Asia's figure). Mosques call five times a day. Open-fronted bars and restaurants, their terraces open as long as they are.
