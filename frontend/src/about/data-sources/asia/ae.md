## Roads

No per-road traffic counts are published. [Dubai Pulse](https://www.dubaipulse.gov.ae/) has none, and the Abu Dhabi portals are not accessible.

Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world estimate per lane; secondary and tertiary roads follow the trips made by the buildings within 5 km, streets the trips their buildings make, else class defaults ([world defaults](/about/data-preparation)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 0.5 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 2.3 % of cars, rolling noise only (IEA 2024).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 80 km/h in towns, 100 outside (cars drive 84 on a two-way one), a motorway 120.

## Railways

Train counts come from the [Dubai RTA timetable on Dubai Pulse](https://www.dubaipulse.gov.ae/dataset/73765e8f-e8c4-443c-9687-288072ed9d12/resource/11515bd3-bdba-466f-ab65-f057bd123ab5/download/gtfs.7z). No current feed was available; the 2025 archive is used, with service up to 31 December 2025. Only the Dubai Tram is taken from it.

Surface metro sections are included; see the [railway method](/about/data-preparation).

Etihad Rail publishes no timetable. Its lines use the [world railway defaults](/about/data-preparation).

## Industry

- Power plants: [Global Power Plant Database](https://datasets.wri.org/dataset/globalpowerplantdatabase), last updated in 2021; newer plants are missing.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: Global Energy Monitor trackers.
- Refineries, smelters and the Jebel Ali port area: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.

## Ships

[Global Fishing Watch](https://globalfishingwatch.org/our-apis/) AIS data, which has no class for yachts and pleasure boats.

## Homes and places

Homes: an air conditioner in every home, running 12.8 hours a day. Mosques call five times a day.
