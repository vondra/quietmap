## Roads

No open traffic counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world estimate per lane; secondary and tertiary roads follow the trips made by the buildings within 5 km, streets the trips their buildings make, else class defaults ([world defaults](/about/data-preparation)).

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 0.1 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023); electric cars 0.8 % of cars, rolling noise only (IEA 2024).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 60 km/h in towns, 80 outside (cars drive 68 on a two-way one).

## Railways

No timetable is loaded. All track mapped in OpenStreetMap uses the [world railway defaults](/about/data-preparation).

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, no wind farms.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Other industrial areas: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (a world value). Mosques call five times a day.
