Measured road traffic covers three countries: federal counts on US highways and arterials, counts with a measured truck share on Mexican federal and state highways, and provincial counts for Quebec. Uncounted streets carry the trips their buildings make, uncounted secondary and tertiary roads the trips made within 5 km of them; uncounted motorways, trunks and primaries follow one model of the trips made around them in the United States and Mexico, and keep class defaults in Canada, Central America and the Caribbean. US roads carry their state's mix of lorries (FHWA, table VM-4).

Rail timetables: Amtrak, VIA Rail, seven Canadian city systems and Mexico City. Freight railways publish no schedules, so a main line takes 20 freight trains a day; one without a timetable also takes 80 passenger trains. Locomotives sound their horns at the level crossings of the US and Canadian inventories.

Wind turbines in the US and Canada are OpenStreetMap's with the rated power and hub height of the national registers, which add 1,610 and 110 turbines OpenStreetMap lacks. Power plants in Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean come from Global Energy Monitor.

Ships: Global Fishing Watch AIS vessel density.
