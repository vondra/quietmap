## Roads

No open traffic counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world estimate per lane; secondary and tertiary roads follow the trips made within 5 km of them, fitted on European counts, else class defaults ([world defaults](/about/data-preparation)).

Where buildings are mapped, residential, living, service and unclassified streets are instead estimated from the buildings they serve.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 43 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 35 km/h in towns, 60 outside.

## Railways

No intercity railway. Surface metro sections are included; see the [railway method](/about/data-preparation).

## Industry

Power plants: Global Energy Monitor, operating plants only, where OSM maps an industrial area at the site. Other industry: OSM industrial areas with a type inferred from name and tags.

## Ships

Global Fishing Watch AIS vessel density.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
