## Roads

The MTC Provías national road network, retained by a [community mirror](https://services6.arcgis.com/G8JFnqCHKQ9vb8YW/), includes observed daily traffic on some sections. A motorway, trunk or primary road takes a counted line within 400 m. A network line without traffic supplies no count. Missing vehicle classes are estimated.

Roads without an applicable count use the [global road model](/about/data-preparation) and the trips generated by mapped buildings. The model does not assign extra traffic from manually drawn city boxes.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 45 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 40 km/h in towns, 60 outside.


## Railways

No timetable is loaded; PeruRail and the Southern Peru copper railway publish no loadable schedule. All lines use the [world railway defaults](/about/data-preparation).

Lima Metro Line 1 takes the light rail default of 80 trains per day where OSM tags it as light rail. Surface metro sections are included; see the [railway method](/about/data-preparation).

## Industry

Mining: a community mirror of the supervised mining data gives outlines of open pits, tailings dams, heap leach pads, waste rock dumps, major concessions and active mining units. An OSM industrial area inside one of them is classed as metal ore mining. Active deposits from the INGEMMET register are attached to the nearest OSM industrial area within 2 km.

Power plants: Global Energy Monitor power tracker, operating plants only, with the same 2 km rule.

These sources only classify areas already mapped in OSM; a pit not mapped as an industrial area is not a noise source.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
