## Roads

No open traffic counts; the transport ministry's map server is unreachable from abroad. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world estimate per lane; secondary and tertiary roads follow the trips made within 5 km of them, fitted on European counts, else class defaults ([world defaults](/about/data-preparation)).

Where buildings are mapped, residential, living, service and unclassified streets are instead estimated from the buildings they serve.

Vehicle mix: where not counted, a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 9.2 % of a town street's traffic (the region's median); electric cars 1.0 % of cars, rolling noise only (IEA 2024).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 45 km/h in towns, 90 outside (cars drive 76 on a two-way one).

## Railways

No timetable is loaded. Every line that OSM maps as a working railway takes the class default: 80 passenger and 20 freight trains per day on a main line, 30 and 5 on a branch. This applies equally to the freight-only Ferrocarril Central, opened in 2023 for the pulp mill at Paso de los Toros, and to little-used inland lines.

## Industry

Power plants: Global Energy Monitor, operating plants only, where OSM maps an industrial area at the site. The three pulp mills at Fray Bentos, Conchillas and Paso de los Toros are therefore classed by their power plants. The La Teja refinery and other sites are OSM industrial areas with a type inferred from name and tags.

Wind turbines are OSM points; a turbine without tagged specs takes the fleet medians, 105 dB(A) at full power and a 105 m hub.

## Ships

Río de la Plata and Atlantic coast: Global Fishing Watch AIS vessel density.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
