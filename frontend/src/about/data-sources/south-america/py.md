## Roads

Road network: the national routes file of the public works ministry MOPC, linked from its [road network page](https://www.mopc.gov.py/red-vial/), 2023 edition. It gives routes and surface but no traffic; no machine-readable counts are published in Paraguay.

An OSM motorway, trunk or primary road within 500 m of a national route is estimated from the surface:

| Surface | Vehicles per day |
|---|---:|
| Paved | 12,000 |
| Part paved, part dirt | 8,000 |
| Dirt | 3,000 |
| Not stated | 6,000 |

The estimate is doubled inside Greater Asunción, its suburbs included, and multiplied by 1.4 in 11 other towns: Ciudad del Este, Encarnación, Pedro Juan Caballero, Concepción, Coronel Oviedo, Villarrica, Pilar, Caaguazú, Filadelfia, Salto del Guairá and Limpio.

These roads take an estimated vehicle mix:

| Where | Light | Medium | Heavy | Motorcycle |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Greater Asunción | 65% | 5% | 10% | 20% |
| The 11 towns | 62% | 6% | 12% | 20% |
| Chaco | 60% | 10% | 20% | 10% |
| Eastern region | 55% | 8% | 25% | 12% |

The Chaco is taken as everything west of 57.3° W and north of 26° S.

Vehicle mix on other roads: a main road takes the medium and heavy shares of counted roads of its class, a street 1 % medium and 2 % heavy; motorcycles 19 % of a town street's traffic (WHO 2023).

Motorways, trunks and primaries that are not a national route use the world estimate per lane. Secondary and tertiary roads follow the trips made within 5 km of them, streets the trips their buildings make, else class defaults ([world defaults](/about/data-preparation)).

Unsigned roads: a primary road 50 km/h in towns, 60 outside.

## Railways

No timetable is loaded. Any stretch that OSM still maps as a working railway uses the [world railway defaults](/about/data-preparation); mapped disused or abandoned lines are excluded.

## Industry

Power plants: Global Energy Monitor power tracker, covering plants in Paraguay and the binational dams Itaipú and Yacyretá. Each is attached to an OSM industrial area within 3 km. Other industry: OSM industrial areas with a type inferred from name and tags.

## Ships

River traffic on the Paraguay and Paraná appears only where Global Fishing Watch AIS vessel density covers it.

## Homes and places

Homes mapped in OpenStreetMap: 16 air conditioners per 100 homes (the region's median). Mosques: only those with a mapped minaret call, once on Fridays.
