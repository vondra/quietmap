## Data situation

South America has no single continent-wide open transport or environmental feed.
National portals, community mirrors, and public registries differ substantially in
coverage and reliability.

## Regional model notes

- Road and rail detail comes from national or regional transport sources where they
  are reachable; class-aware defaults remain explicit elsewhere.
- Industrial coverage combines public facility and power registries with the global
  baseline, while operating status can be incomplete.
- Building heights are often coarser outside major cities, and terrain or land cover
  can dominate uncertainty in large rural and forested areas.

The generated country list on this page identifies the source-specific differences
that matter to the map.
