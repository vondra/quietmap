## Railways

Train counts: no timetable. World default on every OpenStreetMap track: 80 passenger and 20 freight trains per day on main lines, 30 and 5 on branches, 15 freight on industrial track. The single main line, from the South African border through Gaborone to Francistown, is mostly freight and carries far fewer than 100 trains per day; rail noise is overstated.

## Industry

Operating power plants: Global Energy Monitor, matched to OpenStreetMap industrial areas; plants without a mapped area are absent. Other sites are classified from their OpenStreetMap name, otherwise as generic industry.
