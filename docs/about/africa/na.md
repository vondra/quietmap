## Roads

Traffic volumes: no national counts. World defaults by OpenStreetMap class; motorway, trunk and primary are scaled ×0.700, the lower bound. Vehicle and road-length figures are unavailable, so the factor is derived from population density (3.5 people per km², World Bank 2022). Vehicle mix: world default.

## Railways

Train counts: no timetable. World default on every OpenStreetMap track: 80 passenger and 20 freight trains per day on main lines, 30 and 5 on branches, 15 freight on industrial track. TransNamib runs a few freight trains per day on each line; rail noise is overstated.

## Industry

Operating power plants: Global Energy Monitor, matched to OpenStreetMap industrial areas; plants without a mapped area are absent. Other sites are classified from their OpenStreetMap name, otherwise as generic industry.
