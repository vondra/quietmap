## Roads

Traffic volumes: no national counts. World defaults by OpenStreetMap class; motorway, trunk and primary are scaled ×0.957, from 57.4 registered vehicles per kilometre of road (Wikipedia country lists). Vehicle mix: world default.

## Railways

Train counts: no timetable. World default on every OpenStreetMap track: 80 passenger and 20 freight trains per day on main lines, 30 and 5 on branches, 15 freight on industrial track. Zambia Railways and TAZARA run a few trains per day. TAZARA uses the world default (100 trains/day) on the Zambian side and the Tanzanian estimate (3 passenger and 12 freight) across the border.

## Industry

Operating power plants: Global Energy Monitor, matched to OpenStreetMap industrial areas; plants without a mapped area are absent. Other sites are classified from their OpenStreetMap name, otherwise as generic industry.
