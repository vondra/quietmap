## Data situation

Africa has sparse continent-wide transport and environmental data. Public transit
systems and national traffic counts are especially uneven, so the map combines
OpenStreetMap geometry and global layers with the strongest country-level sources
available.

## Regional model notes

- Industrial coverage relies heavily on public power-plant and mine registries,
  including Global Energy Monitor and the Global Power Plant Database.
- Roads and railways use country surveys, corridors, and registries where they are
  available; otherwise the model keeps the source class and applies its documented
  baseline assumptions.
- Building-height and footprint coverage varies substantially. In many areas the
  baseline is coarser than in well-mapped cities, which lowers confidence in local
  screening.

Country pages record meaningful local sources and limitations. The generated country
list on this page remains the single navigation index.
