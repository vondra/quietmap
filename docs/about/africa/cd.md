## Roads

Traffic volumes: no national counts. DR Congo-specific estimates by OpenStreetMap class, scaled ×2.5 in Kinshasa and ×1.4 in 10 other cities, among them Lubumbashi, Mbuji-Mayi, Kisangani and Goma. OpenStreetMap has almost no motorway in the country; the motorway row covers stray tags.

| Road class | Open country | Kinshasa (×2.5) | Other listed cities (×1.4) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Motorway | 15,000 | 37,500 | 21,000 |
| Trunk | 5,000 | 12,500 | 7,000 |
| Primary | 2,500 | 6,250 | 3,500 |
| Secondary | 1,200 | 3,000 | 1,680 |
| Tertiary | 500 | 1,250 | 700 |
| Residential | 250 | 625 | 350 |

Vehicles per day, both directions. Slip roads take half the value of their road. Service roads, tracks and unclassified roads keep the world default.

Vehicle mix: estimated by location. Freight roads are those within 15 km of the Matadi to Kinshasa road or the Kolwezi, Likasi, Lubumbashi, Kasumbalesa road.

## Industry

Operating power plants: Global Energy Monitor, matched to OpenStreetMap industrial areas; plants without a mapped area are absent. Other sites are classified from their OpenStreetMap name, otherwise as generic industry.
