## Railways

Train counts: no timetable. World default on every OpenStreetMap track: 80 passenger and 20 freight trains per day on main lines, 30 and 5 on branches, 15 freight on industrial track. Only the Malaba to Kampala line has regular service, a few trains per day; rail noise is overstated.
