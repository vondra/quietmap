## Railways

Train counts: no timetable. World default on every OpenStreetMap track: 80 passenger and 20 freight trains per day on main lines, 30 and 5 on branches, 15 freight on industrial track. The lines from Antananarivo to Toamasina and Antsirabe and from Fianarantsoa to Manakara run between a few trains per week and a few per day; rail noise is overstated.
