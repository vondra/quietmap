## Railways

Train counts: no timetable. World default on every OpenStreetMap track: 80 passenger and 20 freight trains per day on main lines, 30 and 5 on branches, 15 freight on industrial track. The one line, Abidjan to the Burkina Faso border, runs a few trains per day; rail noise is overstated. The Abidjan metro is not yet open and is not on the map.

## Industry
