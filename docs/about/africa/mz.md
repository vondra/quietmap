## Roads

Traffic volumes: no national counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world estimate per lane; smaller roads use class defaults ([world defaults](/about/methodology)). Vehicle mix: world default.

## Railways

Train counts: no timetable. Mapped active railways use the [world railway defaults](/about/methodology). The separate networks out of Maputo, Beira and Nacala are freight lines with few passenger trains; the defaults overstate them, the passenger count most of all.

## Industry

Operating power plants: Global Energy Monitor, matched to OpenStreetMap industrial areas; plants without a mapped area are absent. Other sites are classified from their OpenStreetMap name, otherwise as generic industry.

## Ships

Coastal traffic: Global Fishing Watch AIS vessel hours.
