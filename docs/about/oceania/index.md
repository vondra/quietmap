## Data situation

Australia and New Zealand provide useful public transport sources; Pacific island
states have much thinner transit and registry coverage. Feed availability and URLs
change more often than the underlying map model, so local exceptions belong on the
country pages.

## Regional model notes

- Australian and New Zealand rail feeds provide additional detail where their
  coverage applies.
- New Zealand and Pacific sources are mixed: some are open and current, while
  others are incomplete or unavailable.
- Island geometry, antimeridian handling, and sparse transport data can matter more
  than the global baseline in smaller states.
