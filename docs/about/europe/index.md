## Environmental Noise Directive (END)

The EU [Environmental Noise Directive 2002/49/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A32002L0049)
requires member states to produce strategic noise maps for major roads, railways,
airports, and agglomerations. quietmap.org uses the END Lden indicator while
extending the atlas to all locations and source classes.

## Regional model notes

European road, railway, and industrial sources use the CNOSSOS-EU framework, with
[ISO 9613-2](https://www.iso.org/standard/74047.html) propagation. Aircraft use an
NPD-based approach informed by ECAC Doc 29, but this is not a certified Doc 29
implementation; see the [aircraft methodology](/about/methodology#aircraft).

Europe generally has stronger public transport and registry coverage than other
regions, but source detail still differs between countries. Country pages record
the local data and the gaps that can affect a result.

## Validation

Public monitoring measurements are the primary validation reference. Official
strategic maps are cross-checks, not calibration targets; see
[methodology](/about/methodology#validation).
