## Roads

National roads: [Transport Infrastructure Ireland](https://data.tii.ie/) counters. Daily traffic is that of one ordinary pre-pandemic weekday, 19 June 2019, in the vehicle classes the counters report. Newer daily files are not openly downloadable.

Dublin also has counts from the [EU city traffic dataset](https://github.com/XavB64/traffic-volume-data-EU-cities). Regional and local roads have no counts. Uncounted streets use the class default, adjusted for surrounding buildings and the counted roads they connect to.

## Railways

Train, DART and Luas counts: [Transport for Ireland timetable](https://www.transportforireland.ie/transitData/Data/GTFS_All.zip), one busy Wednesday. Freight is not covered: the timetable is passenger-only.

## Buildings and terrain

Ireland has open building outlines but no open heights or floor counts. Heights: OpenStreetMap tags and the typical height for the footprint size.
