## Data situation

North America has a strong global baseline and useful national or operator datasets,
but coverage is not uniform across the United States, Canada, Mexico, and the
Caribbean.

## Regional model notes

- US wind turbines use the public USWTDB inventory where its coverage applies.
- Intercity rail enrichment uses public operator data, including Amtrak and VIA
  Rail sources where available.
- Traffic, industrial registries, and building heights still vary by jurisdiction;
  country pages describe the local exceptions.

The country list on this page is generated from the available country pages.
