Measured road traffic covers three countries: federal counts on US highways and arterials, counts with a measured truck share on Mexican federal and state highways, and provincial counts for Quebec. The rest of Canada, Central America and the Caribbean use class defaults.

Rail timetables: Amtrak, VIA Rail, seven Canadian city systems and Mexico City. Freight railways publish no schedules. A main line without a timetable defaults to 80 passenger and 20 freight trains per day.

Wind turbines in the US and Canada come from the national turbine registers. Power plants in Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean come from Global Energy Monitor.

Ships: Global Fishing Watch AIS vessel density.

Layer construction is described on the [methodology page](/about/methodology).
