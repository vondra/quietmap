## Roads

No open traffic counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world default × 0.711. Wikipedia's lists give no vehicles per road kilometre for Oman, so the factor is derived from population density (15 people per km², World Bank 2022). The factor is limited to 0.7–1.3.

| Road class | Vehicles per day |
|---|---:|
| Motorway | 21,330 |
| Trunk | 10,665 |
| Primary | 6,399 |
| Secondary | 3,000 |
| Tertiary | 800 |
| Residential | 500 |

Local streets: traffic is derived from the buildings served; motorcycles 1% (floor), from 0.4 × the two-wheeler share of registered vehicles (WHO 2023 country profile, 2021 fleet).

## Railways

Oman has no railway; the layer is empty.

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, no wind farms.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Other industrial areas: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.
