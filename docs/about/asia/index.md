## Data situation

Asia has no single continent-wide transport or environmental data regime. National
feeds vary in format, openness, and update cycle; some require registration or are
not published at all. The map therefore combines global terrain, land-cover, and
building layers with country-level traffic, rail, aircraft, and industrial sources.

## Regional model notes

- Dense metropolitan areas often have richer traffic and rail inputs than rural or
  politically closed regions.
- Industrial sources are supplemented by public power-plant, mine, and facility
  registries, with coverage and operating status varying by country.
- Building heights and wind-turbine inventories are uneven outside their strongest
  source regions. Country pages explain the consequential gaps rather than hiding
  them behind a continent-wide coverage claim.
