## Roads

No open traffic counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world default × 1.165. Wikipedia's lists give no vehicles per road kilometre for Timor-Leste, so the factor is derived from population density (92 people per km², World Bank 2022). The factor is limited to 0.7–1.3.

| Road class | Vehicles per day |
|---|---:|
| Motorway | 34,950 |
| Trunk | 17,475 |
| Primary | 10,485 |
| Secondary | 3,000 |
| Tertiary | 800 |
| Residential | 500 |

Local streets: traffic is derived from the buildings served; motorcycles 31%, from 0.4 × the two-wheeler share of registered vehicles (WHO 2023 country profile, 2021 fleet).

## Railways

Timor-Leste has no railway; the layer is empty.
