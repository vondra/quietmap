## Roads

No open traffic counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world default × 1.203, from 113 vehicles per kilometre of road (Wikipedia fleet and road-length lists; factor limited to 0.7–1.3).

| Road class | Vehicles per day |

| Trunk | 18,045 |
| Primary | 10,827 |
| Secondary | 3,000 |
| Tertiary | 800 |

Local streets: traffic is derived from the buildings served; motorcycles 2%, from 0.4 × the two-wheeler share of registered vehicles (WHO 2023 country profile, 2021 fleet).

## Railways

Ulaanbaatar Railway publishes no timetable feed. The Trans-Mongolian line uses class defaults: 80 passenger and 20 freight trains per day on main lines, 30 and 5 on branches, 15 freight on industrial sidings.

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, no wind farms.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Other industrial areas: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.
