## Roads

No open traffic counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world default × 1.123, from 86 vehicles per kilometre of road (Wikipedia fleet and road-length lists; factor limited to 0.7–1.3).

| Road class | Vehicles per day |
|---|---:|
| Motorway | 33,690 |
| Trunk | 16,845 |
| Primary | 10,107 |
| Secondary | 3,000 |
| Tertiary | 800 |
| Residential | 500 |

Local streets: traffic is derived from the buildings served; motorcycles 3%, from 0.4 × the two-wheeler share of registered vehicles (WHO 2023 country profile, 2021 fleet).

## Railways

Lebanon has no working railway. Disused track still tagged as rail in OpenStreetMap gets the class default; the click panel shows where this applies.

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, no wind farms.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Other industrial areas: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.
