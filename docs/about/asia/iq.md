## Roads

Iraq publishes no open traffic counts. Traffic is assigned by road class and city:

| Road class | Open country | 14 provincial cities (×1.3) | Basra, Erbil, Sulaymaniyah (×1.6) | Baghdad (×2.5) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Motorway | 40,000 | 52,000 | 64,000 | 100,000 |
| Trunk | 18,000 | 23,400 | 28,800 | 45,000 |
| Primary | 9,000 | 11,700 | 14,400 | 22,500 |
| Secondary | 4,500 | 5,850 | 7,200 | 11,250 |
| Tertiary | 2,000 | 2,600 | 3,200 | 5,000 |
| Residential | 800 | 1,040 | 1,280 | 2,000 |

Each city is a box around its centre, not its administrative boundary.

| Where | Cars | Medium | Heavy | Motorcycles |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Baghdad | 60% | 10% | 22% | 8% |
| Other cities | 58% | 8% | 26% | 8% |
| Open country | 50% | 5% | 38% | 7% |
| Within 25 km of the Basra oil route | 35% | 3% | 58% | 4% |

The oil route runs from West Qurna through Rumaila, Zubair and Basra towards Faw. Its 58% heavy share is an assumption, not a count.

## Railways

No timetable. Track within 12 km of the Baghdad to Basra line gets 3 passenger and 4 freight trains per day; all other track gets 1 and 1. Parts of the network are not in service.

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, wind farms excluded.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Oil fields and refineries: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.
