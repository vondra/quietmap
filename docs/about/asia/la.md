## Roads

No open traffic counts. Motorways, trunk and primary roads use the world default × 1.299, from 576 vehicles per kilometre of road (Wikipedia fleet and road-length lists; factor limited to 0.7–1.3).

| Road class | Vehicles per day |
|---|---:|
| Motorway | 38,970 |
| Trunk | 19,485 |
| Primary | 11,691 |
| Secondary | 3,000 |
| Tertiary | 800 |
| Residential | 500 |

Local streets: traffic is derived from the buildings served; motorcycles 30%, a value taken from Vietnam and Thailand because the WHO profile for Laos has no usable breakdown.

## Railways

The Laos-China Railway publishes no timetable feed and uses class defaults: 80 passenger and 20 freight trains per day on main lines, 30 and 5 on branches, 15 freight on industrial sidings.

## Industry

- Power plants: Global Energy Monitor integrated power list, operating units only, no wind farms.
- Steel works, cement plants, coal mines: GEM trackers.
- Other industrial areas: OSM polygons with a generic sound level.
